Sponsors

Put your name on the robot

Local sponsors are how a competition robotics team gets built, funded, and to the tournament. Here is exactly what that buys and what you get.

Why sponsor

Every dollar turns into hardware and hours.

Sponsoring Millis Dynamics puts your business in front of the whole town — and puts tools in the hands of students who want to be engineers.

Your name goes on the back of the team shirt worn at every event, on the banner in our pit, in the engineering notebook that judges read, and on this website. At the top tier, it goes on the robot itself.

Our goal for the season is $5,500. Any amount moves that number, and we will tell you exactly what yours paid for. Sponsor at $100 or more and your name goes on the back of the team shirt — and you can pay at whatever pace suits you.

Email us See the tiers

What your support pays for

  • The robot — kit, structure, motors, and the control system
  • FIRST registration and qualifier entry fees
  • Travel to events around Massachusetts
  • Team shirts, pit setup, and notebook printing
  • Spare parts, because things break at the worst time

Get in touch and we will walk you through how to send a contribution and what it covers.

Sponsorship tiers

Pick a level

Every sponsor at $100 or more goes on the back of the team shirt. Each tier includes everything in the tier below it, and custom amounts are welcome.

Pay at your own pace. All at once, monthly, quarterly, or split across the season — whatever works for your budget. Tell us what is comfortable and we will set up a schedule around it. Your name goes up as soon as you commit, not when the last payment lands.

Support that isn’t a cheque

Some of the most useful sponsorship a robotics team gets never shows up as money:

  • Materials, machining, 3D printing, or shop time
  • Somewhere to meet and leave a half-built robot
  • Transportation to Saturday tournaments
  • Food for a team that has been at an event since 7am
