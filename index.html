What the team does

Four jobs, one robot.

A VEX team is more than the people holding the controllers. Whatever you are good at — or want to get good at — there is a seat for it.

Build

Cut and assemble the structure, mount the motors, tune a drivetrain that survives a full day of matches.

Program

Write the autonomous routine in VEXcode — blocks, Python, or C++ — and squeeze points out of fifteen seconds.

Design in CAD

Model the robot before a single part gets cut, then iterate the design when the game punishes your first idea.

Document & promote

Keep the engineering notebook judges read at every event, and run the team’s outreach and sponsorship.

The season

From the workshop to Worlds

Every VEX team in the world plays the same game each season. Win enough, document your design well enough, and the path runs all the way to the world championship.

  1. Fall – winter

    Tournaments

    Head-to-head qualifying matches at events across Massachusetts, plus skills challenges ranked against teams worldwide.

  2. Late winter

    Massachusetts State Championship

    The best teams in the state, and the awards that come with a strong engineering notebook.

  3. Spring

    VEX Worlds

    Teams that qualify go on to the world championship — thousands of teams from more than fifty countries.
