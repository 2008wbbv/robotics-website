What this is

A real engineering team, run by students.

FIRST Tech Challenge gives every team the same game and the same deadline. You design a robot, build it, program it, and compete with it — and everything about how you get there is up to you.

Build a competition robot

Design it, machine and print the parts, wire the control system, and drive it in front of a crowd. One season, one robot, real deadlines.

Learn the actual tools

CAD in Onshape, Java on the robot, an engineering notebook that documents every decision — the same workflow used in industry, plus free training from FIRST.

No experience required

Builders, programmers, designers, writers, and people who like organizing things all have a place. Rookie teams are built out of people who show up.

Where we stand

Honest status report

This team doesn't exist yet. Here's exactly how far along the launch is — and what's still open.

  • Researched. We've talked to veteran teams, a rookie team that just launched, and the FIRST Massachusetts program staff.
  • Budgeted. A full year-one budget with sourced prices: about $4,650 lean, $8,570 for a fuller build.
  • Calendar mapped. Kickoff, qualifier windows, and states are all pinned down.
  • Interest survey. Nearly finished — going out through the school this month.

Still open: one community mentor, a place to meet, school sign-off on travel, and the first sponsors.

Help right now

Three things that would move this forward today

Students: fill out the interest form. Three committed people is enough to launch a rookie team.

Parents & neighbors: if you tinker, build, code, or engineer anything, be our mentor. It's a few hours a week and FIRST trains you for free.

Local businesses: sponsor a kit or an entry fee. Your name goes on the shirt and on this site.
