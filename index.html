What the team does

Four jobs, one robot.

An FTC team is more than the two people holding the controllers. Whatever you are good at — or want to get good at — there is a seat for it.

Build

Cut and assemble the structure, mount the motors, wire the control hub, and tune a drivetrain that survives a full day of matches.

Program

Write the robot code in Blocks or Java, and squeeze every point you can out of the thirty-second autonomous period.

Design in CAD

Model the robot in Onshape before a single part gets cut, then iterate when the game punishes your first idea.

Document & promote

Keep the engineering notebook judges read at every event — it is how teams win the Inspire Award — and run outreach and sponsorship.

The season

From the workshop to the championship

Every FTC team in the world plays the same game each season, revealed at kickoff in September and competed straight through the winter.

  1. Dec – Mar

    Qualifying tournaments

    Massachusetts events run all winter, and they cluster east of Worcester — generally under an hour from Millis.

  2. Late March

    Massachusetts State Championship

    The best teams in the state, and the judged awards that come with a strong engineering notebook.

  3. Spring

    FIRST Championship

    Teams that advance out of states go on to the world championship, against teams from dozens of countries.
