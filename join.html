Join / Volunteer

Be one of the first

Rookie teams are made of the handful of people who said yes before there was anything to see. Students, mentors, and sponsors all start here.

Students

No experience needed. Build, program, design, write the engineering notebook, or run outreach and sponsorship. Fill out the form below — it takes two minutes and tells us which meeting times work for you.

Mentors & volunteers

Our single biggest need. You don't need to know FTC — FIRST provides free mentor training, students do the building, and the commitment is a few hours a week. Parents, alumni, neighbors, and anyone who makes things are all welcome.

Sponsors

About $5,500 covers a full rookie season. Sponsor a kit, an entry fee, or whatever fits — your name goes on the back of the team shirt and on this site, and we'll tell you exactly which budget line you covered.

Interest form

Sign up

One short form for everyone — students, parents, potential mentors, and businesses. Tell us who you are and how you'd like to be involved, and we'll follow up directly.

Setup needed

The interest form isn't connected yet

If you're setting this site up, connect the Google Form here:

  1. Open the form in Google Forms and click Publish (or Send).
  2. Choose the embed <> option and copy the src="…" URL.
  3. Paste it into assets/js/config.js as interestForm.embedUrl, and put the plain share link in interestForm.shareUrl.

What happens next

After you sign up

  • We email you. Directly, from a real person — not an automated list.
  • We schedule around you. Meeting times are set from what students pick on the form.
  • First meeting. Once there's a mentor and a space, we start — kickoff is September 12.
  • No obligation. Signing up is saying you're interested, not signing a contract.

Under 18? Have a parent or guardian take a look at the form with you — we'll need their contact info before any meetings start.

Spread the word

Know somebody who'd be into this?

The hardest part of starting a team isn't the robot — it's finding the first few people. If you know a Millis student who'd love this, an engineer who'd mentor, or a local business owner who'd sponsor, send them this page.
