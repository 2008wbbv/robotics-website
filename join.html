Volunteers

Just email us

There is no form for volunteers. Send us a note and we will take it from there.

Useful things to include:

  • What you would like to help with, or what you are good at
  • Roughly how much time you have
  • Whether you are a parent, an alum, or somebody local who just likes building things
Email us See the open roles

You do not need to know FTC

Students design and build the robot. Volunteers are the experienced adults in the room: ask good questions, help somebody find the right tool, ride along to a tournament.

FIRST publishes free mentor training if you want it, and driving, food at events, fundraising, and photography all count as real help.

email us

What happens next

After you sign up

  • We email you. Directly, from a real person, not an automated list.
  • We schedule around you. Meeting times come from what students pick on the form.
  • You show up. Bring yourself. We will tell you ahead of time if you need anything else.
  • No obligation. Signing up says you are interested, not that you signed a contract.

Under 18? Fill the form out with a parent or guardian, since we will need their contact details before meetings start.
