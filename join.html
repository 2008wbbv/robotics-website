What happens next

After you sign up

  • We email you. Directly, from a real person — not an automated list.
  • We schedule around you. Meeting times come from what students pick on the form.
  • You show up. Bring nothing. Everything you need is already in the room.
  • No obligation. Signing up says you are interested, not that you signed a contract.

Under 18? Fill the form out with a parent or guardian — we will need their contact details before meetings start.
