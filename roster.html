Leadership

Who runs what

Everyone on the team works on the robot. These are the jobs people take on in addition to that, and every one of them is open right now. You do not need experience to take one on.

President Open for this season

Runs the team, owns the plan, and is the person the school and sponsors talk to.

Engineering lead Open for this season

Owns the robot: the build, the code, and the design decisions behind both.

Social media lead Open for this season

Runs the team’s accounts, posts from build sessions and competitions, and keeps the team visible.

Fundraising lead Open for this season

Owns the money coming in: sponsors, fundraisers, and following up with businesses.

Plus 2 team members, both open.

Outreach & recruiting lead Open for this season

Brings new students in and gets the team in front of the town.

Plus 2 team members, both open.

Administrative helper Open for this season

Paperwork, registration, forms, and the admin nobody else wants to pick up.
