Who runs what on Millis Dynamics, and which seats are still open for the 2026–27 season.

Leads

Four jobs that run the season

Every one of these is a real job with real ownership. They are open right now, and you do not need experience to take one. You need to show up.

Build lead Open for this season

Owns the chassis, the mechanisms, and everything that bolts together.

Programming lead Open for this season

Owns the robot code, the autonomous routine, and the driver controls.

Design lead Open for this season

Owns the CAD model and the design decisions before anything gets cut.

Notebook & outreach lead Open for this season

Owns the engineering notebook judges read, plus sponsors and social.
