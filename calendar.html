Setup needed

The team calendar isn't connected yet

Once we have a meeting schedule, a public Google Calendar goes here. If you're setting this site up, it takes about two minutes:

  1. Create the team calendar in Google Calendar.
  2. Open Settings → (your calendar) → Access permissions and tick Make available to public.
  3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy the src="…" URL out of the embed code.
  4. Paste it into assets/js/config.js as calendar.embedUrl.

Season milestones

The 2026–27 FTC calendar

  • Open now Team registration Registration for the season is open. The $350 national fee can be paid as late as the morning of our first competition.
  • Sat, Sep 12, 2026 Kickoff — the game is revealed The season's challenge drops. Worth attending even if a team isn't fully formed yet.
  • Sep – Nov 2026 Design and build season Prototyping, CAD, programming, and the engineering notebook. The busiest stretch of the year.
  • Dec 2026 – Mar 2027 Massachusetts qualifiers Roughly ten events statewide; each team is offered two. Entry is $200 per event, mostly east of Worcester and generally under an hour from Millis.
  • Late Mar 2027 Massachusetts State Championship $300 entry, for teams that advance out of qualifiers.
Meeting times

Not locked in yet

We're picking meeting times around what students actually choose on the interest survey — before school, after school, or weekends.

For scale: a highly competitive FTC team meets four evenings a week plus a weekend day in season. A minimum-viable team meets for one hour a week. Year one lands somewhere in between.
