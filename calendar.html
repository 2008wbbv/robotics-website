The team calendar goes here

Meeting times are being set now. If you are setting this site up, connecting the calendar takes about two minutes:

  1. Create the team calendar in Google Calendar.
  2. Open Settings → (your calendar) → Access permissions and tick Make available to public.
  3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy the src="…" URL out of the embed code.
  4. Paste it into assets/js/config.js as calendar.embedUrl.

Season milestones

The VEX season

  • Now Sign-ups are open Students and volunteers can sign up any time — the sooner the better.
  • September Meetings begin Strategy, prototyping, and learning the tools. Times are set around what students pick on the sign-up form.
  • Fall – winter Tournaments across Massachusetts Event dates are posted on RobotEvents as they are scheduled, and land on this calendar as we enter them.
  • Late winter Massachusetts State Championship For teams that qualify through the season.
  • Spring VEX World Championship The end of the season, and the reveal of next year’s game.
