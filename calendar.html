The team calendar goes here

Meeting times are being set now. If you are setting this site up, connecting the calendar takes about two minutes:

  1. Create the team calendar in Google Calendar.
  2. Open Settings → (your calendar) → Access permissions and tick Make available to public.
  3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy the src="…" URL out of the embed code.
  4. Paste it into assets/js/config.js as calendar.embedUrl.

Season milestones

The 2026–27 FTC season

  • Now Sign-ups are open Students and volunteers can sign up any time, the sooner the better.
  • Sat, Sep 12, 2026 Kickoff: the game is revealed The season’s challenge drops and the build season starts the same day.
  • Sep – Nov 2026 Design and build season Prototyping, CAD, programming, and the engineering notebook. The busiest stretch of the year.
  • Dec 2026 – Mar 2027 Massachusetts qualifiers Each team is offered two qualifying events. They cluster east of Worcester, generally under an hour from Millis.
  • Late Mar 2027 Massachusetts State Championship For teams that advance out of the qualifiers.
